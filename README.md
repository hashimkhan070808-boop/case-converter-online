# Case Converter Online

A free online case converter tool. Convert text between 20 case styles instantly, right in your browser — no signup, no uploads, nothing leaves your device.

🔗 **Live tool:** https://onlineconvertercase.com/

## Features

- **20 converters in one place:**
  - Upper, Lower, Title, Sentence, Proper, Toggle, Alternate, Random
  - Camel, Pascal, Snake, Dot, Hyphen, Train, Constant case
  - Remove Spaces, Remove Enter, Reverse Text, Remove Emoji, Remove HTML Tags
- **Convert only the selected part** of your text, not the whole document
- **Copy or download** the result in one click
- **100% private** — everything runs locally in your browser; your text is never uploaded to any server
- **Free forever, no signup**

## Who is it for?

Writers, bloggers, students, creators, SEO professionals, and developers — anyone who converts text between cases multiple times a day, e.g. Python/SQL `snake_case` to JavaScript `camelCase`, CSS `kebab-case`, or constants in `SCREAMING_SNAKE_CASE`.

## How to use

1. Open https://onlineconvertercase.com/
2. Paste your text
3. Click any case style — the result appears instantly
4. Copy or download it

## Privacy

This tool runs entirely client-side. No account, no tracking of your pasted text, no uploads.
